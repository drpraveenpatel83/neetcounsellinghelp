About Us

Empowering medical aspirants with transparent, accurate, and timely counselling guidance.

Who We Are

NEET Counselling Help is India's most trusted and comprehensive platform for medical admission guidance. We specialize in simplifying the complex NEET UG counselling process for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, and Veterinary courses across both All India Quota (AIQ) and State Quota channels.

Founded by medical professionals who have successfully navigated the system themselves, our platform aims to democratize information, ensuring that every student makes an informed decision regarding their future medical college, regardless of their geographical location or background.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate the stress and confusion associated with medical counselling. We strive to provide real-time updates, past cut-offs, fee structures, and personalized guidance through our free tools and community resources.

We believe that a student's hard-earned rank should never be compromised due to a lack of knowledge about counselling rules, form-filling mistakes, or hidden college fees.
